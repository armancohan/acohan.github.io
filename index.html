About

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science at Yale University, where I lead the Yale NLP Lab. Before Yale, I was a Research Scientist at the Allen Institute for AI (AI2).

My research spans problems at the intersection of NLP and machine learning, including large language models, representation learning, retrieval and knowledge-intensive systems, and applications in specialized domains. Currently, I am particularly interested in an in-depth understanding of LLMs and their capabilities, from both evaluation and interpretability perspectives. I am also interested in developing reasoning systems for knowledge-intensive and specialized environments, and my lab builds LLM-based systems that help scientists and other domain experts.

For current areas of focus, the research group, and Ph.D. student openings, please visit the lab website and its opportunities page.
